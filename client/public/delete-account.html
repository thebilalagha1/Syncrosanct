Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 5, 2026

If you'd like to delete your Syncrosanct account and associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ibilalagha@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Syncrosanct account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. We will confirm your identity and process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile information (name, email address) obtained via Google Sign-In
  • All events, settings, and other content you created within the app
  • Your active session data

What may be retained

We may retain minimal records (such as basic transaction or security logs) for a limited period where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Any such data is kept only as long as necessary for these purposes and is not used for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to ibilalagha@gmail.com.
