Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Syncrosanct ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and Android application.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture, as provided by Google's sign-in service.
  • Content you create: Any events, settings, or other data you enter while using Syncrosanct is stored so it can be shown back to you across sessions and devices.
  • Session data: We use a session cookie to keep you signed in, which expires automatically after 30 days.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To save and display your data (such as events and settings) back to you
  • To maintain your signed-in session

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not run analytics tracking on your usage.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate the service itself (for example, Google's sign-in infrastructure) or if required by law.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our servers and protected with reasonable technical measures. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Your Choices

  • You can sign out at any time, which ends your session.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Syncrosanct is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
